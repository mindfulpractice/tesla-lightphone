Tesla for Light Phone

Complete these steps on this computer, then scan one QR code on your Light Phone.

1
Create a Tesla Developer App

Go to developer.tesla.com and create an application with these settings:

  • Allowed Origin: https://tesla-lightphone.app
  • Allowed Redirect URI: https://tesla-lightphone.app/setup
  • API Scopes: select Vehicle Commands, Vehicle Charging Management, and Vehicle Device Data

Copy your Client ID and Client Secret from the app dashboard.

Need more detail?

1. Sign in at developer.tesla.com with your Tesla account.
2. Click "Create Application".
3. Fill in any name and description (these are for your reference).
4. Set the Origin and Redirect URI exactly as shown above.
5. Under API scopes, check the three boxes listed above.
6. Submit the application. It may take a few minutes to be approved.
7. Once approved, copy the Client ID and Client Secret.

Enter Your Credentials
3
Pair Virtual Key

This lets the Light Phone app send commands to your car. Click the button below — Tesla will ask you to approve through the Tesla app on your smartphone.

Make sure you have the Tesla app installed on your smartphone (not the Light Phone).

Pair Virtual Key
4
Sign In with Tesla

Sign into your Tesla account to authorize the Light Phone app. You'll be redirected to Tesla and then back here.

5
Scan QR Codes on Light Phone

Open the Tesla v2 app on your Light Phone and tap Scan QR Code. You will scan two codes:

QR 1 of 2 — Credentials

Scan this first, then the app will ask for the second code.

QR 2 of 2 — Sign-In Token

Scan this second code to complete setup.

Privacy: Everything happens in your browser. Your credentials and tokens are never sent to any server other than Tesla's. You can verify this in the source code.
